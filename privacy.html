Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MAB AI Strategies LLC (“MAB AI Strategies,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you visit our website, contact us, or use our consulting, automation, and AI-related services.

1. Information We collect

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name, company name, email address, phone number, and any details you submit through forms, email, calendar bookings, or other communications.
  • Business information you choose to share with us about your operations, systems, workflows, goals, and project requirements.
  • Website usage information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, pages viewed, and approximate location data derived from IP address.
  • Payment and billing information processed through third-party payment providers; we do not store full payment card numbers on our own servers.

2. How We use information

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule calls, send proposals, and provide requested services.
  • To deliver consulting, implementation, automation, and support services.
  • To operate, secure, maintain, and improve our website, systems, and service delivery.
  • To send administrative messages, invoices, service notices, and other business communications.
  • To comply with legal obligations, enforce agreements, and protect our rights, clients, and systems.

3. How We share information

We may share information with trusted service providers and subprocessors that help us operate our business, including providers for website hosting, payment processing, scheduling, email, analytics, CRM, cloud infrastructure, and productivity tools. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect legal rights, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

4. Data retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal, tax, accounting, or compliance requirements.

5. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to understand website traffic, improve site performance, and support basic website functionality. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, though disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

6. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or limitation of certain personal information we hold about you. To make a privacy-related request, contact us using the information below.

8. Third-party services and links

Our website and services may contain links to third-party websites, tools, and integrations. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties, and you should review their policies separately.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for business users and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above. Your continued use of our website or services after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
