About

Understand the system. Then automate it.

MAB AI Strategies builds the agents, workflows and data plumbing that take repetitive work off your team, then measures the result in hours and dollars.

Founder & CEO · Principal AI Architect

Mark Bockrath

Mark spent his career leading enterprise sales teams before building AI systems full time. He kept seeing the same thing: talented people spending their week being human middleware between systems.

He started MAB AI Strategies to fix that directly. No slide decks and no software licenses to resell. The firm builds the actual system, wires it into the tools you already run, and measures what it gives back.

Strategy, sales and delivery are one chain here. If a plan can't survive implementation, it isn't done. If a build doesn't move revenue, cost, risk or speed, it isn't the first thing to build.

How an engagement runs

  1. Find the bottleneck. We look at where the hours go before anyone talks about tools.
  2. Design around your stack. Your CRM, inbox and spreadsheets stay. The system fits them.
  3. Automate the part that's worth it. Fixed logic stays fixed. AI handles the judgment calls, with a person approving the ones that matter.
  4. Measure it. Hours returned and dollars moved, reported in plain numbers.

Vendor-neutral by design

Every tool is chosen for your infrastructure, security posture and budget. If you can solve most of the problem with what you already own, that's what we'll tell you.
